## 📋 ABSOLUTE MUST-KNOW RULES

**Format**
- ✅ Markdown (.md) file
- ✅ ONLY bullet points (NO paragraphs)
- ✅ Each bullet: 15-25 words maximum
- ✅ 3-5 bullets per section maximum

**Structure**
- ✅ # = Title (use once only)
- ✅ ## = Section topics (~15 total)
- ✅ ~15 H2 headings = ~15 slides

**Content Distribution**
- ✅ Results: 4,000-5,000 words (PRIORITY - 32-35% of total)
- ✅ Introduction: 1,400 words (10%)
- ✅ Methodology: 2,400 words (17%)
- ✅ Discussion: 2,800 words (20%)
- ✅ Everything else: remaining words
- ✅ TOTAL: 12,000-14,500 words

**Critical: Every Result Must Include**
- ✅ Specific metric (percentage, mean, p-value, effect size)
- ✅ Example: "78% of participants (n=331/425) improved (p < 0.001)"

## 🔥 TROUBLESHOOTING HOTLINE (3 Most Common Issues)

### Issue 1: Output Exceeds 15,000 Words
**Quick Fix (send this to AI)**:
```
"Output is [X] words. Please trim:
1. Literature Review: reduce to 1,700 words
2. Discussion: reduce to 2,700 words
Keep Results section completely unchanged.
Recount and provide exact new word count."
```
**Time to fix**: 3-5 minutes (AI re-runs)

### Issue 2: Results Section Missing Specific Metrics
**Quick Fix (send this to AI)**:
```
"Each finding in Results needs:
- Exact percentage or mean value
- Statistical significance (p-value if available)
- Sample size or count of participants

Example format: 78% (n=331/425) showed improvement (p < 0.001)

Revise entire Results section to include these metrics for all findings."
```
**Time to fix**: 3-5 minutes

### Issue 3: Structure Incorrect / Wrong Number of H2 Headings
**Quick Fix (send this to AI)**:
```
"Reorganize document so there are exactly 15 H2 headings (##).
Use NO H3 (###) headings.
Each ## heading represents approximately one slide topic.

Current issues: [list what's wrong]
Needed structure: [describe what's needed]

Please fix and show me the revised structure."
```
**Time to fix**: 2-3 minutes

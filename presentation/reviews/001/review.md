## **STRICT RECOMMENDATIONS TO BRIDGE THE GAP**

### **1. Signal the 50k Word Depth**

Since you cannot fit 50,000 words on slides, you must use **Academic Signposting**.

- **Action:** Add small footers or parenthetical notes like `(See Thesis Section 4.3 for full Comparative Matrix)` or `(Source: Systematic Review Data, Chapter 3)`.
- **Reason:** This tells the committee, "I know this slide is a summary, and the proof is in the document you are holding".[10][11]

### **2. Technical "Proof of Work" (The SLR Slide)**

The committee will grill you on how you chose the 234 sources.

- **Action:** Replace or expand Slide 3. Add the **Search String** you used (e.g., `TITLE-ABS-KEY("Maritime" AND "IoT")`) and a small diagram showing how you narrowed 1,000+ results down to 234.[7][12]
- **Reason:** In an SLR thesis, the _process_ of selection is as important as the _result_.[6]

### **3. Fix the "Borrowed Credibility" Issue**

The impressive percentages (92% delivery, 40% safety) look "made up" or "copied from a brochure" without a citation on the same slide.[2]

- **Action:** On Slides 8 and 9, add the specific author/year for those stats.
- **Example:** "On-time Delivery 92% [Zhang et al., 2023]".[2]

### **4. Address the "Garbled" and Contextless Data**

Even if the 50k word thesis explains them, "broken" slides suggest a lack of attention to detail.[8]

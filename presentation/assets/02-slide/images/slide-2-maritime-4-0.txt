A sophisticated, dual-pane conceptual visualization for a technical maritime engineering presentation. 

Left Pane (Current Industry State): A minimalist architectural schematic of a conventional vessel bridge. Overlaid icons represent "Human-Centric Vulnerabilities," with subtle heatmaps indicating cognitive load and fatigue points. Statistical data callouts (75-96% human-error coefficient) are integrated into the design using clean, professional typography. The color palette uses muted greys and cautionary ambers to signify systemic inefficiency and digital fragmentation.

Right Pane (Maritime 4.0 Ecosystem): A high-fidelity technical rendering of an autonomous-ready vessel within a "Connected Sea" framework. The image features a complex mesh of digital twin overlays, real-time telemetry nodes, and satellite-to-ship data links (VDES/6G icons). The visual emphasis is on "Systemic Optimization" and "Data-Driven Safety." The color palette is composed of deep navies, precision whites, and technological cyans, conveying a sense of stability, precision, and environmental stewardship.

Style: Technical blueprint aesthetic combined with realistic 3D rendering. Clean, flat design elements; high-contrast but professional; 8k resolution; suitable for a high-level academic defense or industry whitepaper. No cinematic drama; focus on structural clarity and information density.
